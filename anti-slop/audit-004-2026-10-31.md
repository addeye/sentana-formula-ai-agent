# Anti-slop audit 004

Date: 2026-10-31
Scope: standard common icons and purposeful micro-motion added to Tailwind CSS and Bootstrap 5 templates in `skills/sentana-ui-patterns/templates/`.
Mode: AFTER, follow-up audit for icon and animation additions.
Direction: restrained business admin workspace, navy/teal semantic palette, dials ENERGY 1 / RHYTHM 1 / MOTION 2.

## Findings

No HIGH or MEDIUM defects found.

1. **LOW: Icon styling distinction between frameworks preserved intentionally (R-04).** Tailwind templates use outline Heroicons/Lucide inline SVGs (`stroke="currentColor"`, `w-4 h-4` / `w-5 h-5`), while Bootstrap 5 templates use official Bootstrap Icons inline SVGs (`fill="currentColor"`, 16x16 `bi bi-*`). Both frameworks use content-relevant symbols with `aria-hidden="true"` and preserve explicit text labels.
2. **LOW: Motion dial calibrated to level 2 micro-motion (R-19).** Micro-motion is restricted to mobile drawer entry (0.2s slide-in/fade), loading skeleton pulse (`@keyframes pulse`), and subtle interactive hover/focus transitions (`transition: all 0.15s ease`). All motion is stripped under `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)`.

## Purposeful Motion & Reduced Motion (R-19 Review)

- **Sidebar Drawer (<768px):** Uses native `<dialog id="navigation-dialog">`. On `[open]`, applies `drawerSlideIn` (0.2s `ease-out`, `translateX(-100%)` to `0`) and `backdropFadeIn` (0.2s `ease-out`, `opacity: 0` to `1`).
- **Skeleton Loading State:** `@keyframes pulse` alternates opacity between 1.0 and 0.4 over 1.8s `ease-in-out infinite` to signal ongoing operation without layout shift.
- **Interactive Controls:** Subtle 0.15s ease transitions applied to buttons, links, and `.demo-edit` on hover and focus.
- **Accessibility & Motion Preference:** `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` disables all transitions and animations (`transition: none !important; animation: none !important;`).
